Material Spotlight

Sintered stone worktops, built to last

For countertops and island tops, we work extensively with sintered stone — a dense, low-porosity surface that resists heat, scratching and staining far better than standard quartz.

It gives a clean, seamless finish with none of the joint lines or upkeep that natural stone demands, so the island stays the centrepiece of the kitchen for years, not just the first year.

  • Heat and scratch resistant surface
  • Non-porous — resists staining, no sealing needed
  • Seamless joints for a clean, continuous look

FAQ

Good to know before you book a visit

What areas do you cover? +
We work on projects across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, including Puchong, Subang, Petaling Jaya and the wider Klang Valley.
How long does a full kitchen fit-out take? +
Most kitchens run 4–8 weeks from confirmed design to handover, depending on scope and material lead times. Wardrobes and smaller built-ins are typically quicker.
Do you handle both design and installation? +
Yes — one team carries the project from the first site visit through 3D design, fabrication and on-site installation, so nothing gets lost between handoffs.
Is the first site visit free? +
Yes, the initial site visit and measurement are complimentary before you confirm a project with us.
